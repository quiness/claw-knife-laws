Knife Laws by State

Verified statutes and plain-English summaries for every US state. Updated for 2025.

Knife laws vary widely across the United States. Some states like Texas and Ohio have full preemption with no restrictions on switchblades or automatics, while states like California and New York heavily regulate blade types and concealed carry.
